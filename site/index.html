As easy as one, two, three

  1. 1

    Open any app or website

    Mail, Safari, System Settings, a website you're visiting: anything you'd like help with.

  2. 2

    Press ⌃ ⇧ Space

    Nudge looks at the window and tells you what it's for.

  3. 3

    Follow the highlight

    Nudge points at each button and explains it. Press Space for the next one.

Big, clear and calm

Every explanation arrives on a large, easy-to-read card. A soft glow shows exactly where to look, and everything else is gently dimmed, so your eyes always know where to go.

Nudge floats right beside what he's explaining, and keeps clear of the thing he's talking about.

What Nudge can do

  • Explains the whole window

    First what the window is for, then its buttons, links, fields and menus, in order. On a website, Nudge says where each link goes.

  • Explains just one part

    Press ⌃ ⇧ A and drag a box around anything. Nudge explains only what's inside: its buttons, its pictures, then its words.

  • Sees pictures

    Inside a box you drag out, Nudge looks at photos and pictures and tells you what they seem to show: "That's a photo of a dog."

  • Helps you get something done

    Type what you want to do, like "print this page", and Nudge shows you one step at a time, waiting for you after each one.

  • Reads to you

    Nudge can read every card aloud, and you can pause or stop him anytime. Press Space to go on, at your own pace.

  • Keeps things simple

    Buttons that belong together are explained together. A calculator's number keys are one step, not ten.

  • Asks how he did

    At the end, Nudge asks "Did I do good?" If something wasn't right, you can tell him. Your answer stays on your Mac.

  • Looks the way you like

    Choose the colors, the font, how big the text is, the highlight, the glow, and how big Nudge is, with a live preview.

Meet Nudge

He points at whatever he's explaining, thinks while he reads, cheers when you're done, and hides his eyes when he gets something wrong.

Your screen never leaves your Mac

Nudge is built so that it can't share what you're looking at.

  • No internet needed. Nudge has no servers, no accounts, no ads and no tracking.
  • Private by design. Explanations come from Apple's own on-device intelligence, right on your Mac.
  • Only when you ask. Nudge looks at a window only after you press its shortcut, and never reads password fields.
  • You stay in charge. Nudge shows you where to go. He never clicks, types or changes anything for you.
